## 2026-11-22 — Turn the Game > My Points page into an engaging dashboard (#173)

The My Points page used to pour four tables onto the screen one after another.
Now each section is its own card ("pod") with a soft blue gradient and rounded
corners, showing just the key numbers: your total and whether points are final;
your finished tasks, their points and the last finish date; how many scoring
rules there are and how many are active; and your latest engagements, the
points in them and the newest one. On a wide screen the pods sit side by side;
on a phone they stack.

Each pod with a table has an **Explore** link. It opens the full table in a
pop-up that covers nearly the whole screen, with much tighter rows so more fit
at once. Close, Escape or a click on the dark background shuts it. Error and
"nothing here yet" messages kept their wording and now show inside their own
pod. Nothing about how points are counted changed.
