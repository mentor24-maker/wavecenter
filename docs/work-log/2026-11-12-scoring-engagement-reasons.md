## 2026-11-12 — Nobody can read why every engagement earns points: write Dane's reasons (#163)

Dane decided that every form of human engagement, chatting included, earns
automatic points, and said why the worry about people gaming the system for
points does not hold: members are chosen for wanting to produce value, the
small automatic points are not worth chasing next to the big points for
substance and quality, and the system will watch for gaming and penalise
it. Until now that lived only in a chat. The scoring wiki now carries his
words and the three reasons, on the Principles and Anti-farming pages, and
the open question about penalties notes that he has confirmed they will
exist (what they look like is still his to decide). It matters because the
chat feature is about to get its scoring rules, and the next session that
designs one will read this instead of arguing the opposite again.
