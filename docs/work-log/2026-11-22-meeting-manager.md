## 2026-11-22 — Add a Meeting Manager to the Teams page (#177)

The Teams page now has a Meeting Manager beside the list of teams. Pick a
team, and a month calendar shows which dates already have a meeting. Click
any date and a pop-up opens: it shows that day's meetings (when they start,
where they happen, such as Zoom or Google Meet, notes, and who is invited),
and lets a member of the team plan a new one or change one.

Dane chose who may do this: any member of a team plans its meetings and may
invite anyone in WaveCenter. Everyone else can see the meetings but not
change them, and the database itself enforces that, so nobody can get around
the screen. An invitation is recorded on the meeting; no message is sent yet.
Times are Mountain time.

This is the first piece of scheduling in WaveCenter, a step toward the
project-management tool: teams can now agree when and where they meet,
inside the same platform where their work is tracked.
