## 2026-11-10 — Make home page icons larger/brighter (#161)

The small round icons at the top of each box on the Home page were easy to
miss. They are now twice as big and a dark but bright aquamarine with a white
drawing, so each box stands out at a glance. The same colour works in light
and dark mode, and the white drawing clears the readability bar for icons
(3.37 to 1, against a minimum of 3 to 1).

The aquamarine has its own name in the style sheet, so links, buttons and the
icons on other pages are untouched. Checked on Home at desktop width in light
and dark mode, and at phone width: all seven boxes show the bigger badge with
the heading centred beside it.
